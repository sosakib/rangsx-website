# Privacy Policy
Last updated: June 2026
Rangs Motors Ltd operates this website to inform prospective customers about our electric vehicles in Bangladesh. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, and how we keep it safe. By using this website you agree to the practices described below.
## 1. Information We Collect
We collect information in two ways:
Information you provide directly — your name, phone number, and email address when you submit a lead form, book a test ride, or reach out via WhatsApp.
Information collected automatically — pages visited, browser type, device category, and approximate city-level location, gathered through standard web analytics tools. We do not collect precise GPS coordinates.
## 2. How We Use Your Information
To respond to your product enquiries and arrange test ride appointments.
To share relevant updates about RangsX vehicles, offers, and events — only with your prior consent where required.
To improve our website experience based on aggregated, anonymised usage data.
To comply with legal obligations under applicable Bangladeshi law.
## 3. WhatsApp Communication
If you contact us through WhatsApp, your messages are processed on WhatsApp's platform under their terms of service. We use WhatsApp solely to respond to customer enquiries. Your number is never shared with third parties for marketing purposes.
## 4. Sharing Your Information
We do not sell or rent your personal data. We may share it only with:
Authorised RangsX dealers — to follow up on your enquiry or arrange a test ride in your area.
Service providers — website hosting and analytics partners who process data on our behalf under strict confidentiality agreements.
Regulatory authorities — where we are legally required to do so.
## 5. Data Retention
We retain your contact details for up to 24 months from your last interaction with us. After that period, data is securely deleted unless a longer retention period is required by law.
## 6. Your Rights
You have the right to:
Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
Request deletion of your personal data, subject to any legal obligations.
Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time.
To exercise any of these rights, contact us at
[email protected] . We will respond within 14 business days.
## 7. Security
We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure. However, no internet transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
## 8. Cookies
This website uses essential cookies that are necessary for the site to function, and optional analytics cookies that help us understand how visitors use the site. You can disable non-essential cookies through your browser settings at any time. Essential cookies cannot be turned off without affecting site functionality.
## 9. Children's Privacy
This website is not directed at children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, please contact us so we can promptly delete it.
## 10. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of the website after a revision constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

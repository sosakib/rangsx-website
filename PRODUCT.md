# Product

Derived from the client's own words in `scrollcraft/builds/rangsx-home/BRIEF.md` and
`progress.md` (22-27 Sep 2026). Edit freely; every design task reads this first.

## Register

brand

## Users
Bangladeshi buyers choosing an electric vehicle, in two lanes:
- Riders and families looking at RX electric scooters (ES3, ZS, T60): mostly on phones, comparing range, price and
  where to get service.
- Business owners and fleet managers looking at Dongfeng electric cargo vans (EM-26) and microbuses (EM-27):
  comparing running costs against diesel/CNG and asking for a quote.
Their job: understand the offer fast, trust that Rangs Group stands behind it, then call, book a test ride, or find
a dealer.

## Product Purpose
The rangsx.com marketing site: present RangsX as the EV ecosystem for Bangladeshi roads (vehicles, service, support)
and turn interest into leads (test rides, fleet quotes, dealer visits). It keeps the beta site's page tree and
per-page layouts, with calmer, more premium visuals, motion and interaction. Success = a visitor reaches the right
vehicle and a contact action without friction, on a phone.

## Brand Book
RANGSX brand book (Documents\RANGSX_BRANDBOOK_compressed.pdf). Promise: "Powering Purposeful Progress". Positioning: modern,
reliable, green electric mobility. Archetype: the Innovative Everyman (60/40 Everyman/Creator). Pillars: Problem
Solver, Go Green, Cost Intelligent, Smart Urban Style, Resourceful and Efficient. Colours: red #ED2328, black, grey
#BCBEC0, white. Type: LEMON MILK (English), Li Ador Noirrit (Bangla), Poppins (body). Taglines: "Electromobility"
(category line, with the logo), "Drive Next" (RangsX campaigns), "Ride Beyond" (RX bikes only).
Bangla voice (sample key messaging, p.16): everyday English loanwords written in Bangla (ইলেকট্রিক ফ্লিট, স্মার্ট,
সার্ভিসিং), short and confident.

## Brand Personality
Calm, proud, local. Premium without being cold; confident, never loud. Client words: "more sophisticated, minimal,
and attractive", "user-interactive", animated elements that feel smooth and relevant.

## Anti-references
- A single long landing page: the site must stay multi-page, like the beta.
- Red glows that turn pink in light mode: light mode uses grey glows, red only for accents.
- Long scroll-jacked intros: the homepage opening must stay short.
- Generic EV-startup neon and stock "futuristic" chrome.

## Design Principles
1. Same map as the beta: every page and section the beta has, in the same order.
2. The product is the hero: real vehicle cut-outs and real specs, never decoration in their place.
3. Motion explains, then gets out of the way; everything works with reduced motion.
4. Phone first: most buyers arrive on a phone in Dhaka traffic, on mobile data.
5. Every page ends in a way to act: call 16758, test ride, dealer, quote.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA. Reduced-motion users get a static, stacked version of every scroll effect. Colour is never the only
signal (division filters, open/closed status). Every page also exists in Bangla at /bn/...; keep copy plain in both.
